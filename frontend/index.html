“DON'T JUST RESPOND TO DISASTERS. PREPARE FOR THEM.”

ResQNet integrates real-time weather forecasting with Open-Meteo API to classify rainfall risk zones, predict flood hazards, and recommend safe shelter evacuation routes before disaster strikes.

ResQNet is a hackathon prototype and is not a replacement for official emergency services. Real-world deployment would require emergency-service integration, security validation, privacy compliance, reliability testing, professional disaster-management review, and regulatory approval.
